\paragraph{Scope.} B3 (64$\times$384$\times$384) has two speeds: in most reps it drops into a slow state
(\iss{36}, \iss{41}, \iss{42}). \iss{43} tried one fix: before each pack, clear the pack-buffer lines with
\texttt{cbo.zero}, so the packer's stores would not have to read those lines from the L2 first. This brief covers
what was built and 168 board launches: 7 rounds on \texttt{jbpi} and 7 on \texttt{jbpi2}. Each round is 12 launches,
switch off/on in the order off, on, on, off, off, on. \textbf{Every speed number here is from the uninstrumented
build} (\texttt{INSTRUMENTED=0}), reference protocol, \texttt{--tmin 10}, core 4. Speed is in GOPs, by mean time
(the reference's formula) and by median rep. Every log cited is committed (Appendix~A).

\paragraph{Cases} (all tile 16$\times$15, config \texttt{config\_issue43\_zero\_lines.json}):
B3\_nc128 = 64$\times$384$\times$384 at the shipped nc=128;
B3 = the same shape at the model's nc=384;
B3\_K256 = 64$\times$384$\times$256;
B2 = 384$\times$384$\times$64;
Reuse24 = 384$\times$384$\times$384.

\section{Bottom line}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{B3\_nc128 (64$\times$384$\times$384, nc=128):} \good{about 6.0 GOPs with the switch, against about
  5.6 without}, by mean time, on both boards (by median rep: about 6.1 against 5.0--5.4). On \texttt{jbpi2} the switch
  is higher in all 7 rounds. On \texttt{jbpi} it is higher in 4 of 7: in the other 3 rounds the ``off'' launches
  were themselves already at 5.7--6.1.
  \item \textbf{Reuse24 (384$\times$384$\times$384):} \good{about 8.35 GOPs against about 8.0}, higher in 13 of 14
  rounds.
  \item \textbf{B2 (384$\times$384$\times$64):} \down{about 12.25 GOPs against about 12.40}, lower in 14 of 14
  rounds. A small step, but it happens every time.
  \item \textbf{B3 at nc=384 and B3\_K256:} no change, about 4.5--4.7 GOPs either way.
  \item \textbf{Why it helps is open.} The counters (\S4) show the switch does \emph{not} remove L2 reads: the
  pack's L2 stores rise by about one per line cleared. The next step is a control with the same loop but
  no \texttt{cbo.zero}. Turning the switch on by default is the user's decision.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{How to read it.} With the switch on, B3\_nc128 lands at 5.7--6.2 GOPs in every round on both boards
except round 1 on \texttt{jbpi} (6.79). Without it, \texttt{jbpi2} sits at 5.5--5.7, while \texttt{jbpi} is mostly
at 5.5 but reached 5.7--6.1 in rounds 3, 4 and 7. So the switch mostly lifts the low ``off'' level up to where
\texttt{jbpi} sometimes gets on its own.

The pack's L2 stores rise by about one per line cleared, and its L2 loads never fall. \texttt{cbo.zero} on the X60
seems to write the zero line out to the L2 rather than just claim it in the L1. So the gain is not from saved reads.
The instrumented build's own speed also moves the other way for B3\_nc128 (not a number to quote), which is one more
sign that the switch changes timing rather than traffic.

\midrule
\texttt{templates/pack\_lines.h} & Finds the whole 64-byte lines inside a byte range (partly written lines at either
end are left alone) and clears them with \texttt{cbo.zero}, written as its encoding so the build needs no Zicboz
\texttt{-march}. Does nothing on the host. \\
Generated dispatch & \texttt{if (zero\_pack\_lines)} clears the Bc block and the Ac block just before each pack,
inside the pack timer. Both packers; AXPY only; the DOT path is unchanged. \\
Driver, Makefile, script & \texttt{--zero-pack-lines}, off by default, passed through like \texttt{--scalar-pack}:
its own \texttt{\_zeroline} log names, a ``Pack lines'' line in the run overview and \texttt{provenance.txt};
refused for DOT kernels. \\
\texttt{reps\_report.py} & A launch with the switch is its own row (\texttt{vector+zeroline}). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\textbf{Checks:} host tests of the line finder, the real generated dispatch (GEMM right, lines cleared only with the
switch on), the flag, log names and script; on \texttt{jbpi}, the real \texttt{cbo.zero} before each RVV packer gives
the scalar packer's layout for every shipped panel width. Full suite: 283 passed. Reviewed on two axes (standards,
spec) before commit.
