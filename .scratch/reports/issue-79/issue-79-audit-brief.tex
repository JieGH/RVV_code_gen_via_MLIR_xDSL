\paragraph{Scope.} An independent audit of the YOLO work: \iss{58} (parent), \iss{63} (S6, best tile per shape at 8
threads, the result for H\'ector) and \iss{56} (S5, the parallel engine \iss{63} runs on). Three parts, each with its
own report in \texttt{.scratch/reports/issue-79/}: \textbf{A}, fit to the request (\texttt{A-request-fit.md});
\textbf{B}, the numbers (\texttt{B-numbers.md}, output in \texttt{B-recompute-output.md}); \textbf{C}, the engine

uninstrumented, reference protocol, \texttt{-{}-tmin 2}, \texttt{-{}-no-nc-overrides}; ours 8 threads on cores 0--7
(\texttt{OMP\_PROC\_BIND=close}), 1 thread on core 4; his \texttt{gemm.x} at \texttt{b292c92}, 8 threads on cores
0--7, no binding. GOPs by mean time, packing included, one pass over the 50 YOLO26 shapes.

$n$\,=\,16--64; MR\,=\,16 with NR\,=\,12--15 for $n \ge 80$. Closest kernels in his library: 32x8, 16x16 (and 16x4 at $n$\,=\,4).

\section*{The two anomalies (causes named, not proven)}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{His Y00} (76800x16x27) varies 15.8x across 9 committed launches; no other shape of his moves more than
1.56x. Y00 has 300 \texttt{ic} blocks, so 300 barriers per call (next highest: 19), and it is the first call of
each process. Test: run \texttt{gemm.x} with Y00 moved to a later row.
\item \textbf{Our slow reps at 8 threads}: 2--10\% of reps on mid-size shapes cost a roughly fixed extra 8--13\,ms and
take 30--46\% of the time. Core 0 spent 13.1\% of its time in the kernel during \iss{63}'s runs against 0.2--0.5\% on
cores 1--7, and our threads are bound, so the thread on core 0 can't move. Test: 7 threads on cores 1--7, or binding off.
\end{itemize}
