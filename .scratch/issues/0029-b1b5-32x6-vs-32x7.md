## Question
B1 (1024x384x1024) and B5 (1024x384x4096) ship with `32x6`. That tile was set by the issue #2 test plan to
match prev_xdsl, not by a speed test. #37 showed `32x7` beats `32x6` on B4 and S1-S5. Is `32x7` also faster
for B1 and B5?

## Setup
- `datasets/b1b5_32x7.dat` (new): B1 and B5 at 32x7. The 32x6 side is the shipped `issue2_bert_32x6.dat`.
- `config_b1b5_nr7_first.json` and `config_b1b5_nr6_first.json` (new): the same two rows in opposite
  order, both `skip_ref_run=true`, `iterations=1000` (same knobs as the shipped 32x6 row).
- 4 launches in A-B-B-A order (7-first, 6-first, 6-first, 7-first; order checked from the log times), so
  unlike #37 the run order is balanced.
- `compile_macro_bench.sh --jump --riscv-ip jbpi2 --no-nc-overrides --cpu 4 --tmin 10`: uninstrumented,
  reference protocol, core 4. Board checked before each launch: no other user's programs; core 4 busy share
  99-100%, every other core 0-2%, in every launch.
- 99-102 reps per launch for B1, 24-25 for B5; std-dev under 0.5% of the median in every row.

## Result
GOPs by mean time, 32x6 / 32x7 (by median rep matches to within 0.01 GOPs in every cell):

| Case | L1 (7 first) | L2 (6 first) | L3 (6 first) | L4 (7 first) | Average | 32x7 ahead |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | 7.96 / **8.12** | 7.95 / **8.14** | 8.02 / **8.14** | 7.95 / **8.09** | 7.97 / **8.12** | +1.9% (every launch: +1.5 to +2.4%) |
| B5 | 7.68 / **7.83** | **7.68** / 7.66 | 7.70 / **7.74** | 7.71 / **7.78** | 7.69 / 7.75 | +0.8% (−0.3 to +2.0%) |

- **B1: 32x7 wins in all 4 launches**, by 1.5-2.4%. Small, under the ~3% between-launch noise, but each gap
  is read inside one launch and the sign never changes, with the order balanced.
- **B5: level.** 32x7 is ahead on average by 0.8%, but lost launch 2 (by 0.3%). By the rule set in advance
  (switch only if it wins every launch), B5 does not change.
- Both gaps are much smaller than on B4 (+3.7%) and the square shapes (+6.6 to +8.7%) in #37. That fits
  N=384 splitting evenly into tiles of 6 (64 tiles) but not of 7 (54 full + one edge tile), though the
  reason was not measured.

## Limits
- One board (`jbpi2`). The plan was to add `jbpi` since both gaps are under 5%; it was busy.
- The reference was not run (`skip_ref_run`).

## Decision (user, 2026-09-28)
**On hold.** No change to the kernel dimensions: B1 and B5 stay at 32x6 in `config.json`, which also keeps
the matched-tile comparison with prev_xdsl. The configs and dataset stay in the repo to rerun this later
(for example on `jbpi` once it is free).
