true reference kernel would give; do not quote one as 'the reference number'. The two neighbours are
not equally close: 32x8 and 16x16 hold 32 C accumulators plus the A registers, more than the 32 vector
registers there are, so they spill and ran at about half the speed of 32x6 and 16x14 in the first
reference run. The lower-NR row is the nearer one.

  - The three sets were not made in the same hours; time of day and board temperature are not controlled.

  - The reference writes its GFLOPS with 3 significant digits, so its figures carry a rounding of about 0.1%.
